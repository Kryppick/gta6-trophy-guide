Roadmap to the Platinum

Play in this order. The story alone takes around 60 hours; the full platinum is roughly 200 hours, according to the launch guides. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.

    Missable trophies — read this first

    Only one trophy is flagged as possibly missable by the launch guides: Positive Mental Attitude. The meditation spot on Lucia’s balcony in Chapter 1 is reported to disappear once that chapter closes, so meditate there before Chapter 1 ends. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam. When in doubt, keep a manual save per chapter.

      How this guide is built

      Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list as published on 18 November 2026 by Exophase and PSNProfiles, cross-checked by Planet GTA 6, Output Lag, GTA Vibes and GTA Six How. Each guide is written in my own words for this entry and names its sources beside it — a source is cited, never copied. Where the game has only just launched and guides do not yet agree on an exact trigger, the guide says so instead of guessing. This is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with Rockstar Games. It loads no Rockstar artwork and makes no network request: it runs entirely from its own files.
